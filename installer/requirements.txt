Tailout uses FFmpeg and FFprobe to read, preview and process videos.

Install FFmpeg 6 or newer with the libx265 encoder before using Tailout.
Both ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe must be on PATH, or placed in the same folder
as tailout.exe. These tools are not included in this installer.

Windows builds are linked from https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
NVIDIA encoding also needs a compatible NVIDIA GPU and driver.

Tailout installs for your Windows account without administrator privileges.
Upgrading or uninstalling preserves your saved preferences, presets and videos.
Close Tailout before upgrading so an active export is never interrupted.
